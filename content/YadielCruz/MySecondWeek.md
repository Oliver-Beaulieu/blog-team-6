### Individual Contributions

In Phase II, I was mainly responsible for the portion that dealt with the CS and database design. I helped develop the localized ER models for each persona that we have including our Policy Analyst, Humanitarian Coordinator, and Climate Displaced Student personas. In the design I included the entities, relationships, and cardinalities for each localized model and then this helped take all three personas and put them into one which helped develop the global ER model for the TERRA application.

Additionally, I also contributed to developing the first SQL DDL for the global data model. This includes putting the ER model into relational tables and also I had to define the different primary and foreign key relationships in our data. Also, we created the initial structure for entities such as countries, climate indicators, climate events, displacement, policies, NGOs, and risk assessments which are still subject to change but for right now it’s good hard. Finally, Hamza worked on the wireframes but I helped in the sense of mapping out possible features and layouts that could be included in the interface for each user personas.
