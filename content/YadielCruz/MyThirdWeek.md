## Contribution
For Phase III I contributed by focusing on creating the backend REST API routes and the front-end UI for Diana’s Humanitarian Coordinator persona. For the backend I worked on most of the routes except for the risk classification route including countries, climate events, and saved views. These routes help support the app’s feature by comparing countries, viewing climate events, and also saving comparison views.

On the front end, I focused on Diana’s screen and redesigned her Humanitarian Coordinator home page so that it worked more like a dashboard instead of just a list of buttons. I added sections including monitoring humanitarian risk, checking priority countries, managing NGO support, and exporting country summaries. Also helped create her sidebar so her pages connected clearly.
