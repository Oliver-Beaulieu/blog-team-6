# Contributions
I did most of the draft for the second ML model, which is a logistic regression classification ML model that predicts whether a country-year has a high number of asylum applications based on the categories from the CSV file (population, urban pct, dry days, year, precip total, heatwave days, unemployment rate, temp mean, precip days heavy, gdp per capita, and evapotrans total). I used the CSV file that I cleaned back in phase 2 to train this model. 
I also made a prototype model (that never got pushed to main) which was also a logistic regression classification ML model that predicted a country-year's climate risk level (low, medium, and high) based on the categories from the cleaned CSV file from phase 2 (population, urban pct, dry days, year, precip total, heatwave days, unemployment rate, temp mean, precip days heavy, gdp per capita, and evapotrans total), although I noticed the output looked off, so I've decided to possibly work on that model at a later date.
I also created the logo for TERRA, going for a simple, minamilist design.

# Program Related
Over the last week or so, I went to Ghent, Strasbourg, and Luxembourg. 
In Ghent I saw the Mystic Lamb in Saint Bavo's Cathedral and went to Gravensteen Castle. I enjoyed both quite a lot, as the architecture and paintings of the cathedral were breath taking to look at, while Gravensteen Castle had a funny audio comedy tour.
In Strasbourg I explored the giant church we kept passing by and saw the giant clock in it. I also went to a lot of restaraunts, and enjoyed the food there.
In Luxembourg I saw Eurostat, and it was cool to see how they operated. I also had some good food, like the Cordon Bleu.
