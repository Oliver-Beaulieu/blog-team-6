# Hamza's Individual Deliverable

## What I've Learned

One of the most fascinating things I've learned about is the **European External Action Service** — the EU's diplomatic service, which coordinates foreign policy and manages relations with non-EU countries. Learning how the EU projects a unified voice globally gave me a new appreciation for **international diplomacy**.

## My Contribution to Phase 1

For Phase 1, I contributed to **defining our project topic** and going to develop **two user personas** to ground our research in real perspectives.

## Life in Leuven

Leuven has been super cool. Interacting with so many people my age here has been **fascinating**, and the food is spectacular — every cuisine you try tastes amazing.

## NAM-IP Computer Museum

I also visited **NAM-IP**, the computer museum, which was a highlight as a CS student. During the workshop, me, Yadiel, and Tyler **took apart a whole computer** and it was really cool to see all the inner workings and putting everything back together.
