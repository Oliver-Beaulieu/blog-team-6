### Individual Contributions

In Phase II, I was mainly responsible for drafting the wireframes for our TERRA application's proof of concept. I worked through the UI structure for each of our three personas, the Policy Analyst, Humanitarian Coordinator, and Climate Displaced Student, mapping out what each user would actually see and interact with when navigating the app. This involved thinking through the layout of dashboards, data visualizations, and the flow between different screens so that the features lined up with the user stories we had developed in Phase I.
Outside of the wireframes, I also helped Yadiel a little bit with the CS and database design work. While he led the localized ER models and the global ER model for our personas, I helped brainstorm small details that could be missed, such as entities or relationships worth including and how certain features on the UI side might connect back to the underlying data model. It was useful to bounce ideas back and forth since the wireframes and the data model had to line up for the app to make sense as a whole.

### Program Reflection

Outside of class, something that really stood out to me this week was the workshop on NATO, security, and defense led by Major Serge Strobants from Bants Consulting. Getting to hear directly from someone with his background gave a perspective on international security that you can't really get from a textbook or lecture. He talked through how NATO actually operates, the current security and defense landscape in Europe, and the kinds of strategic considerations that go into policy decisions. Being in Belgium, so close to where a lot of these institutions are headquartered, made the whole conversation feel a lot more tangible than it would have back on campus.
