## Tracking European Climate Risk & Refugee Asylum

CS 4973 · Summer 2026 · Northeastern University in Leuven, Belgium

Climate change is displacing people all across Europe. We are building **TERRA** to change this. TERRA will combine climate indicators, disaster records, and displacement statistics across all 27 EU member states into one platform.

> **TERRA** — Yadiel Cruz · Hamza Chakir · James Chan · Oliver Beaulieu*
